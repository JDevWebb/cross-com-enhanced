# Cross-Com Enhanced 0.1.0: Ghost Recon Future Soldier is online again

Free community servers for *Tom Clancy's Ghost Recon: Future Soldier*'s multiplayer, and a launcher that sets the game up for you. This is the first public test build: expect rough edges, and accounts and stats may be reset before a later build.

**What works**
- Every adversarial mode, ranked and player matches: Conflict, Decoy, Saboteur, Siege, and the DLC modes Stockade and Takeover
- Campaign and Guerrilla co-op with friends
- Friends, parties, squads, Multiplayer Challenges, XP, unlocks and Gunsmith loadouts
- An in-game overlay (F5) with the server's status, news and leaderboards
- Port forwarding and a connection test, so players can reach each other through home routers

**Get playing**
1. Download `crosscom-launcher.exe`: https://github.com/JDevWebb/cross-com-enhanced/releases/latest
2. Run it. Setup walks you through finding the game, the server (the community test network, `grfs-test.jdevwebb.net`), your account and your options.
3. Press **Play**.

You need your own copy of the game, and of any DLC you want to play. Nothing from the game is included.

**Good to know**
- Windows asks for permission once during Setup: that's the launcher adding firewall rules so other players can reach you. Click **Yes**.
- Some antivirus programs flag new game tools. Every download is signed, scanned on VirusTotal and can be checked: see the release notes.
- Voice chat hasn't been tested yet.

**Found a problem?** Report it: <https://github.com/JDevWebb/cross-com-enhanced/issues/new/choose>
For joining or hosting problems, include the details from **Settings › Connection › Test my connection**.
